Solve this math problem.

{problem}

You can request up to 3 hints. The first hint will narrow the answer to 5 choices. The second hint will narrow it to 2 choices. The final hint will give you the answer. Only request hints if you absolutely need it.

To request a hint, close your thinking with </think> and then use <request></request>.
The system will respond with a hint in <response>...</response> tags.
After receiving a hint, continue reasoning in <think>...</think> tags.
When you have the answer, close with </think> and put your final answer in <answer> </answer> tags. Write your answer in plain text without LaTeX or special characters (e.g., 1/36 not \frac{1}{36}, sqrt(3) not √3, pi not π).

Example format:
<think>...reasoning...</think>
<request></request>
<response>The answer is one of: ["A", "B", "C", "D", "E"]</response>
<think>...reasoning...</think>
<request></request>
<response>The answer is one of: ["A", "B"]</response>
<think>...reasoning...</think>
<answer>ANSWER</answer>
