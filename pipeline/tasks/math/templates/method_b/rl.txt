Solve this math problem.

{problem}

You have access to a hint system. You can request up to 6 progressive hints. Each hint reveals more of the solution approach. If you get stuck, request a hint.

Do all of your reasoning inside a single <think> </think> block. To request a hint, write <request></request> without leaving the think block.
The system will respond with a hint in <response>...</response> tags, and you continue reasoning in the same think block.
When you have the answer, close with </think> and put your final answer in <answer> </answer> tags. Write your answer in plain text without LaTeX or special characters (e.g., 1/36 not \frac{1}{36}, sqrt(3) not √3, pi not π).

Example format:
<think>
...reasoning...
<request></request>
<response>...hint 1...</response>
...reasoning...
<request></request>
<response>...hint 2...</response>
...reasoning...
</think>
<answer>ANSWER</answer>
